<details><summary>मानसतरङ्गिणीकृत् - टिप्पनी</summary>

The li~Nga-purANa preserves a stava attributed to brahman, that is likely of pAshupata provenance. It encodes a tricephalic iconography of rudra, privileging the sadyojAta face as one of two lateral faces (as opposed to the more widespread version with aghora). The second lateral face is that of vAmadeva, which is typically associated with umA, but that is not made explicit here.  It also gives a hint at the form of the brahma-mantra-s among the exponents of this stava:
