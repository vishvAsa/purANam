विश्वास-प्रस्तुतिः - 6.1

तमथान्धकासुरनिकारविप्लुताः कृतपूर्वसेवमभिराद्धमानसम्।
ऋतवोऽभिलक्ष्यनिजलक्ष्मविग्रहाः शरणं ययुः शतमखेन चोदिताः ॥ 6.1 ॥

मूलम् - 6.1

You, who possess the most subtle vision and omniscience regarding the meaning of all reality, are inferred by the wise through your possession of supreme power – inasmuch as you have made here this wondrous sphere of action (kārya-maṇḍalam), which encompasses the entirety of reality, beginning with the diatomic molecules to the higher molecular linkages.

मानसतरङ्गिणीकृत् - टिप्पनी

In his haravijaya, kavi ratnAkara (whom rAjashekhara calls an oceanic kavi) from the kAshmira country, in a difficult verse, presents shiva as an embodiment of the vaisheShika theory. The pAshupata shaiva-s (the atimArga) were proponents of a shaiva version of the vaisheShika theory where shiva was seen as the agent setting in motion the first interactions between the elementary particles. The wise are said to infer the existence of rudra through the existence of atomic combinations.

O Lord! Never, under any circumstance, and in no place, can an obstruction (upaghAta) occur to your expansion (prasara), by your will (ichChA), [which continuously] gives birth to the brightly and distinctly gleaming (sphurat-sphuTa) bodies (vigraha), the organs (akSha) [of life forms], and the worlds (bhuvana) that are strung together by the unbroken attributes of your shaktis (avipanna-shakti-guNa-gumphita).

मानसतरङ्गिणीकृत् - टिप्पनी

A verse from rAtnAkara’s haravijaya presenting the pervasion of the entire cosmic worlds, the bodies and the organs of organisms by the same unbroken thread of the shakti-s of rudra. This triad “bodies, organs and worlds”, often expressed by the term “tanu-karaNa-bhuvana” is found repeatedly in shaiva thought of both the Urdhva- and dakShiNa-srotas. Here, it is directly seen as the expansion of shiva, which is pervaded by a continuous thread of shakti-s. This unbroken threading by the shakti-s provides the continuity to the universe despite the existence of distinct (sphuTa) bodies, organs and worlds.

मानसतरङ्गिणीकृत्

O Lord! The entire collection of mantra-s has you as its very conscious essence, and the host of gods, in turn, has those mantras as their essence.
Ah! In this threefold world made of the three cosmic qualities (matter, energy, information), there is absolutely nothing that exists separate from you.

मानसतरङ्गिणीकृत् - टिप्पनी

In his hara-vijaya, ratnAkara has a verse summarizing the “siddhAnta” of the shaiva-shAsana.
It lays out the principle for meditating on shiva: the essence of the gods is the collection of mantra-s and their essence in turn is shiva.

The commentators take the threefold world to mean the three realms defined by the mahAvyAhR^iti-s: the earth, the mid-region and deep space or alternatively, the humans, other animals and the gods.

Here, he is essentially elaborating the scriptural verse from the raurava-sUtra-saMgraha, which is cited by the commentators:

sarve mantrAtmakA devAH
sarve mantrAH shivAtmakAH |
shivAtmakam idaM j~nAtvA
